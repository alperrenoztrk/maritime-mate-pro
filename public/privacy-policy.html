1. Overview

Mariner's Book ("the App") is a calculation, education and certificate-tracking tool for maritime professionals and students. This policy explains what data is processed, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and your rights.

4. Protection of Documents

  • Document photos are kept in a private storage bucket and served only to you through short-lived signed URLs.
  • Database access is restricted by Row Level Security: each user can only reach their own rows.
  • All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS; data is encrypted at rest on the server side.
  • Personal fields on the document screen are excluded from the translation service.

5. Retention

  • Account and profile data: until the account is deleted.
  • Documents and document photos: until you delete them or close your account.
  • Quiz results and statistics: until the account is deleted.
  • Purchase records: until the account is deleted. Deleting your account also deletes the purchase record held in the App; payment and invoice records remain subject to Google Play's own retention periods.
  • AI chat history: device-only; removed when you clear app data.
  • AI content reports: until the account is deleted.
  • Server backups: data deleted from the live system is permanently removed from backups within 30 days.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in the App via Settings → Account → "Delete my account". Deletion covers your profile, quiz results, statistics, document records, document photos, uploaded files, user-generated components, usage counters, AI content reports and credentials, and cannot be undone.

If you have a subscription: deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. To stop billing you must also cancel it under Google Play > Subscriptions.

You may also email nauticalleapapps@gmail.com to request deletion or a copy of your data; requests are handled within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data. Where processing is based on consent (documents, location, AI) you may withdraw it at any time: in Settings → Privacy choices for AI and documents, and in your device settings for location. Contact us at the address above; you may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; material changes will be announced here and in the App.
