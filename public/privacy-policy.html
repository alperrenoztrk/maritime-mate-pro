Privacy Policy – Mariner's Book

Effective date: July 3, 2026 · Package: com.maritime.calculator

1. Overview

Mariner's Book ("the App") is a calculation and education tool for maritime professionals and students. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account info: Email address and name, only for signed-in users. An account is optional; calculation features work without one.
  • Usage analytics: None.
  • Location, contacts, microphone: None.
  • Camera/Photos: Used only for actions you initiate (e.g. attaching an image); images are not uploaded to our servers without your consent.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication and content storage. Privacy policy
  • Google Gemini AI: Powers Q&A features; user content is processed transiently and not stored by us. Google privacy policy
  • Google AdMob: May be used to serve ads if advertising is enabled in the App. Ads policy

4. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS.
  • Database access is restricted via Row Level Security (RLS).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account from the Settings section in the App, or request deletion of your data by emailing ozturkalperen308@gmail.com. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
